    Sync your devices

    Connect using your SyncMark username and password. Use HTTPS when connecting over a network.

    Desktop: choose WebDAV (vendor “other”) in rclone, then use bisync for a local offline folder. The repository includes scripts/sync-files.js for selective and scheduled sync with conflict copies.

    Mobile: use a WebDAV sync app with offline folders and camera-upload support. Configure the DCIM/camera folder as a one-way upload pair to a dedicated server folder; enable background access in your device settings.

    Sync and background uploads run in your chosen client. This browser page does not run a background mobile service. WebDAV supports conditional writes, but does not provide DAV locks or Nextcloud-specific client APIs.
