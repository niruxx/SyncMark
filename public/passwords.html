    Unlock your vault

    Enter your vault passphrase to decrypt your saved passwords. This is separate from your SyncMark login password and is never sent to the server.

    Set up your password vault

    Choose a vault passphrase. It encrypts everything client-side before it ever reaches the server — SyncMark never sees it or your unencrypted passwords.

    Save your recovery key

    This is the only way to get back into your vault if you forget your passphrase. Save it somewhere safe — SyncMark cannot show it to you again.

    warning If you lose both your vault passphrase and this recovery key, your saved passwords cannot be recovered by anyone, including SyncMark.

    Recover your vault

    Enter your recovery key and choose a new vault passphrase. A new recovery key will be generated afterward.

    Upgrading your password vault…

    Re-encrypting your existing passwords under your new vault key. This only happens once.

    Change vault passphrase

    Your saved passwords stay exactly as they are — only the passphrase that unlocks them changes.
