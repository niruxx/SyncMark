About

SyncMark is a self-hosted bookmark manager. Import bookmark exports from your browser, then browse, search, edit, and remove them here.

Keyboard shortcuts (on the Bookmarks page): / to search, n to add a bookmark, Esc to close dialogs.

Delete account

This permanently deletes your account and all bookmarks, folders, and settings. There is no undo. Enter your password to confirm.
